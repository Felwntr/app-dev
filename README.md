# Castlevania (Netflix Series)

**Castlevania** is an adult animated *dark fantasy* series based on the classic Konami video game franchise, specifically *Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse*. It ran on Netflix from 2017 to 2021 across **4 seasons**.

## Plot Summary

After Dracula's wife, Lisa, is burned at the stake for witchcraft, he vows to wipe out humanity. A disgraced monster hunter must stop him with the help of unlikely allies.

## Main Characters

1. **Trevor Belmont** - the last of a disgraced family of monster hunters
2. **Sypha Belnades** - a magician and scholar from the Speakers
3. **Alucard** - Dracula's half-human, half-vampire son
4. **Dracula** - the vampire lord seeking revenge on humanity

## Why I Like It

- Amazing fight choreography
- Dark, gothic art style
- Great voice acting
- Well-written story and characters
